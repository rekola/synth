Tokens

Surfaces, text and the accent differ between dark and light. Track colours and the playing and recording states are one set in both themes, because the Launchpad is always dark, has no icons and must show the same colours; playing also stands for queued to play (the glyph differs here, the pad pulses or flashes), recording for queued to record. Every pair meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 glyphs and blocks), checked in plans/modern-look-tokens.md. Clip names are examples.
